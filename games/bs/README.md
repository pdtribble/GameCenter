# BS (Cheat)

A bluffing card game. Play cards face-down claiming they're the required rank. Call other players' bluffs.

## Rules
- Ranks cycle: A → 2 → 3 → ... → K → A
- Play 1–4 cards per turn, claiming the required rank
- Any player may call BS at any time
- Loser of BS challenge picks up the whole pile
- First to empty their hand wins

## Players
- Min: 2, Max: 8

## Settings
- `botDifficulty`: `'easy'` | `'medium'` | `'hard'`
